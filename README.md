# BankCustomer\_Churn

BankCustomer\_Churn Analysis



Dataset Information ontained from :

https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground



Account information for 10,000 customers at a European bank, including details on their credit score, balance, products, and whether they have churned.

Single Table with 13 Fields.



Power BI data standardization/ cleaning and analysis is performed to gain business insight

about What and Why based on attributes available in dataset.



Balance and Credit Segment wise Total, Active and Churn Counts

Customer Segment and Gender wise Total, Active and Churn Counts

Active/Inactive  with Salary range and Churn Count

Active Count and Churn Count by Tenure

Geography, Gender, Product Segment and Credit Segment wise Active and Churn Counts

Geography wise Customer Count and Total Balance distributions.



•	What attributes are more common among churners than non-churners? Can churn be predicted using the variables in the data?

•	What do the overall demographics of the bank's customers look like?

•	Is there a difference between German, French, and Spanish customers in terms of account behavior?

•	What types of segments exist within the bank's customers?
